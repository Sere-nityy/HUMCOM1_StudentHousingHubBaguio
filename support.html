Baguio City Student Guide

Ocular Visit & Rental Safety Checklist

✓ Recommended Before Paying
1. Inspect Water & Rainy-Season Readiness

Check water tank pressure during peak morning hours and inspect ceilings/walls for dampness or mold common during Baguio's wet months.

2. Verify Landlord Identity & Caretaker

Confirm you are speaking with the registered owner or authorized caretaker listed on our verified record before signing any agreement.

3. Clarify Sub-Metering & Curfew Terms

Ask for exact electricity/water computation rules, visitor hours, and gate lockout times in writing before handing over advance rent.

4. Zero Advance Payment Without Viewing

Never send reservation fees via GCash or bank transfer before visiting the room in person or completing a verified live video tour.

System Troubleshooting

Common Website Errors & What They Mean

If you run into a system notice while browsing rooms or submitting forms, check the guide below to understand what happened and how to fix it right away.

📡 CONNECTION LOST

Check Your Internet Connection

Appears when your Wi-Fi or mobile data drops while loading live room filters or photos.

How to fix: Toggle Airplane Mode off/on, check mobile data signal, and refresh the page.

🔍 ERROR 404 • NOT FOUND

Listing or Page Not Found

Happens when a boarding house has already been occupied and removed by admins, or a link was mistyped.

How to fix: Head back to Find Rooms to view active verified listings near your school.

🛡️ ERROR 403 • RESTRICTED

Student Verification Required

Shown if you try to submit an inquiry or access protected landlord details without a valid .edu.ph verification check.

How to fix: Complete the quick check on verify.html with browser cookies enabled.

🛠️ ERROR 500 • SERVER ISSUE

Temporary Database Sync

Occurs briefly when admins are updating room availability timestamps or performing maintenance.

How to fix: Wait 30 seconds and refresh. Do not submit forms twice during a sync.
