### 2.2 Target Audience
*Describe the people who will use your website.*

- **Age range:** 18-30 years old
- **Who they are:** College Students of University of Baguio
- **Devices they use:** Mostly Smartphones, Tablets, Laptops and sometimes Computers.
- **Internet access:** Low Mobile Data
- **Accessibility needs:** Needs large readable texts for the introduction, and normal texts on looking for accomodations along with visible images of preview rooms. Filters and colored texts are also needed to higlight information about the rooms, along with last checked/updated of admin to ensure latest infomration of available accomodations.
- **How this affects my design:** Lightweight page with simple UI along with minimal elements for faster site loading.

### 2.3 Chosen Aesthetic Theme
*How will your website look and feel?*

- **Theme name:** Forest Green
- **3 mood words:** Minimal, Nature, Calm
- **Why this theme fits my users:** Users would usually look for housings so the website should be simple as possible and should not have distracting elements so the attention turns to the housings itself. With simple yet effective UI, users would able to load the website easily without consuming too much mobile data or slowing their device on loading elements.

### 2.4 Interface Goals
*What should users be able to do on your website? Make each goal clear and specific.*

1. Users can look for cheap and affordable accomodations around Baguio along with 3 filters checking Target University, Room Category and Price.
2. Landlords can list their homes clearly and effectively. 
3. Users can load the website with low data and any type of device.
